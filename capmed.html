Interactive Mechanism

How it works

Step through the capsule's four-stage process — from detection to fat elimination.

Step 01 of 05
Swallow the Capsule
CAP-MED-V3 is ingested like any standard oral capsule. Its biocompatible shell navigates the digestive system and localises near fat tissue deposits.
  • Biocompatible outer shell — safe for ingestion
  • Micro-scale design — travels naturally through the body
  • No anaesthesia or surgical preparation needed
Step 02 of 05
Detect Fat Deposits
Onboard optical lenses scan surrounding tissue in real-time to precisely identify and map fat cell clusters, ensuring only targeted fat is treated.
  • High-resolution optical lens array
  • Real-time fat cluster mapping
  • Zero healthy tissue interference
Step 03 of 05
Wireless Power Activation
Energy receptors on the capsule capture wirelessly transmitted near-infrared light or ultrasound signals from an external wearable device — no internal battery required.
  • NIR light or ultrasound energy capture
  • Deep-tissue penetrating wireless power
  • No internal battery — fully wireless operation
Step 04 of 05
Inject Biochemical Reagents
MEMS micro-injectors release precisely measured doses of non-destructive enzymatic agents into targeted fat cell clusters, stimulating intracellular fat metabolism.
  • MEMS micro-injectors for precision dosing
  • Non-destructive biochemical reagents
  • Triggers cellular fat breakdown — not cell lysis
Step 05 of 05
Fat Burns — Body Stays Safe
The biochemical reaction converts fat into CO₂, water, and ATP energy. Healthy muscle, nerve, and surrounding tissue remain completely unharmed throughout the process.
  • Fat → CO₂ + H₂O + ATP (natural metabolic byproducts)
  • Zero damage to nerves, muscle, or surrounding cells
  • No surgical cutting — fully non-invasive
